I am a mathematician working at the interface of probability and algebra. My interests include random walks on groups, random polynomials, probabilistic methods in algebra, and the structure theory of rings and algebras.

Currently, I am a postdoc at Université Paris Dauphine–PSL, working with [Prof. Justin Salez](https://www.ceremade.dauphine.fr/~salez/). Previously, I was a postdoc at the Weizmann Institute of Science with [Prof. Gady Kozma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gady_Kozma). I did my PhD at Bar-Ilan University under the supervision of [Prof. Gideon Amir](https://u.math.biu.ac.il/~amirgi/) and [Prof. Uzi Vishne](https://u.math.biu.ac.il/~vishneu/).
